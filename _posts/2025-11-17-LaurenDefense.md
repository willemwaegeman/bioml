On Monday, November 17th, Lauren Theunissen successfully defended her PhD thesis titled "Addressing Uncertainty in Automatic Cell Type Annotation of Single-Cell Transcriptomics Data". 
Her research focused on translating theoretical uncertainty research into practical applications, specifically in the context of automatic cell type annotation of single-cell transcriptomics data.
We congratulate Lauren on this significant achievement and wish her the best in her future endeavors!
